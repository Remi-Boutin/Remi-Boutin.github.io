I am currently involved in a postdoc at LMBP & FactoLab, Université Clermont Auvergne in partnership with Michelin,
under the supervision of [Anne-Françoise Yao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KBpI8BcAAAAJ&hl=fr).
My research focuses on graph-structured data, as well as graphical modelling and deep neural networks extensions (such as variational graph neural networks). I am currently working on sensitivity analysis and extensions. 

I obained my PhD in 2023 at the [MAP 5 laboratory](https://map5.mi.parisdescartes.fr), 
 [Université Paris Cité](https://u-paris.fr/), for my thesis on _Deep graphical models and inference strategies for the analysis of networks comprising textual edges_
under the supervision of [Pierre Latouche](https://lmbp.uca.fr/~latouche/)
and [Charles Bouveyron](https://math.univ-cotedazur.fr/~cbouveyr/) and pursued in a postdoctorat under the supervision of [Catherine Matias](https://cmatias.perso.math.cnrs.fr/).

My areas of research are related to *statistical network analysis*, *network clustering*,
*topic modelling* as well as *deep probabilistic modelling*. Feel free to send me an email for more information concerning my work.
